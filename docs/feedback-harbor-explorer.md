# Flat look feedback

The `flat` look was a strong fit for this film: solid fills, stable geometry, and the lack of line boil made crowded docks, crane structures, cargo stacks, and small character silhouettes stay legible in motion. The main authoring gotcha was that `look: "flat"` removes render jitter but does not make authored blob geometry perfectly circular, so `ellipse()` and explicit `looseness: 0` still matter for a genuinely precise ligne-claire result. I also wished Tier 0 treated an intentional off-canvas entrance as visible over its animation path; because it evaluates the initial transformed bounds, the arriving steamship had to begin partially in frame to lint cleanly. Film cuts, camera panning, IK walking, and synthesized sound otherwise combined cleanly, and the zero-warning validation loop was especially useful at this scale.
