# Feedback: building "Half Baked" with `look: "clay"`

Notes from building `examples/story/clay-baker.ts` (a ~74s five-scene comic short, `look: "clay"` throughout) cold, from AGENTS.md + the examples.

**The clay look itself is the easiest register I could have been handed for comedy.** The ~10fps time quantization does the comic-timing work for free: an authored hold (the lean-in before the batter explodes, the fly sitting on the cherry, the flour sack's slow tilt) reads as a deliberate stop-motion beat rather than dead air, because the frame is genuinely *held*. Squash-and-settle impacts and pop-in `appear` entrances feel native to the register — props "placed on the set between frames" — so I barely used `drawOn` at all, and the scene didn't miss it. Forced solid fills also meant `sketch.shade()` gradients just worked everywhere with zero fiddling.

**The one real bug-shaped surprise: group transforms without a pivot.** `squashTo`/`rotateTo`/`rotateBy` on a `Group` with no `pivotAt()` transform around the local origin, not the group's bbox center — my flattening title-card cake teleported to the top-right corner of the frame, and a tumbling hat orbited off-screen. The renderer's `transformOrigin: "50% 50%"` fallback apparently doesn't hold for `<g>` elements in the render harness. Plain strokes/blobs are fine. Once known it's a one-line fix per group, but nothing warns about it; I'd want either a computed bbox-center default pivot for groups or a lint that flags a pivotless group being scaled/rotated. Related paper cut: `pivotAt` on a node that also has `initial({x,y})` needs the translate added back by hand (the lantern-maker's comment about this is the only place it's documented — it deserves promotion to the main reference).

**The Tier 0 linter earned its keep, with two frictions.** The IK reach check caught genuinely broken staging (my baker originally stirred a bowl a full counter-width away — every target 2x past max extension) and pushed me to restage him *behind* the counter, which also composed better. But (1) the off-canvas check is error-level with no `lintIgnore` escape, so entrances from off-screen have to be faked (start just inside the frame, or hide with `appear` + an on-canvas `initial`) — I'd like `lintIgnore("off-canvas")` for intentional entrances; and (2) the degenerate-shape check fires on the font's own dot glyphs (i-dots, periods), so every caption had to be written around "i" and "." — the library flagging its own font should probably be exempt.

**What worked great:** the `validate` → `inspect` → `--at` still → `contact-sheet` loop is an excellent agent workflow (every layout bug I found, I found from a rendered still, cheaply); particles are wonderfully cheap and read perfectly under the clay hold (the batter explosion and the flour whiteout are the two best shots in the film); `scene.label()` + `endAt` kept a 20-op collapse sequence retimeable; and the identical-`moveBy` trick from `sketch.walk`'s docs made the carried cake tower track the baker exactly with no rig at all.
